Project Defernius - Narrative Endings & Symbolism

Core Theme:
The game uses a 9-level hellscape structure to explore Jungian shadow work and non-dual realization. The player avatar (Headless Horseman) remains a constant witness across changing environments.

The Boss (The Dark One):
- Represents the Shadow Self / Ego structure.
- High-resolution, half-translucent, half-reflective orb.
- Mechanically, the health bar represents the Ego's resistance.

The Endings:

1. Death in Limbo (Level 1):
  Screen: Black void.
  Text: "It is known."
  Meaning: Blind realization of truth. The player accidentally stumbles into the fact that what is sought is already found (Samsara/Buddha nature without awareness).

2. Death to The Dark One (Level 9 Boss):
  Screen: Blinding white background.
  Text: "You have been liberated."
  Meaning: Dissolution. Losing the fight reframes failure as a release from the struggle/ego. The "unaware self-aware" individual is freed from the burden of trying.

3. Victory (True Ending):
  Text: "The darkness you fight is within"
